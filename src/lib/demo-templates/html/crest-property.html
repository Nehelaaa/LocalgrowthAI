[ About Me ]

Your advocate in every transaction.

For over two decades, I've guided buyers and sellers through Massachusetts' most significant residential transactions — from Beacon Hill townhouses to Nantucket waterfront estates. My practice is built on discretion, deep market knowledge, and white-glove service from first showing through closing.

Before real estate, I studied architectural history at Boston University — a foundation that helps my clients evaluate homes as lasting assets, not just square footage. I maintain a boutique roster so every client receives my full attention, and I leverage an off-market network cultivated across Greater Boston, the Cape, and the Islands.
