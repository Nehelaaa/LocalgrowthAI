Where is {{business_name}} located?+

What services does {{business_name}} offer?+

We offer hair styling and coloring, bridal makeup, eyelash extensions, luxury facials, waxing, spa treatments, manicures, pedicures, and personalized skincare consultations.

How do I book an appointment?+

Book online through our website booking form or call us during salon hours. We recommend booking ahead for bridal, color, and weekend appointments.

Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.

Do you specialize in bridal hair and makeup?+

Yes. Our artists create bespoke bridal hair and makeup looks designed to photograph beautifully and last through your celebration.

Absolutely. Our colorists specialize in dimensional balayage, highlights, and luxury color services tailored to your skin tone and style.
